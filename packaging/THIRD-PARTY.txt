Ski-E-O Map Creator -- third-party software and data
=====================================================

This is a fan-made tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the developers of Ski-E-O!.
No game files are included: the creator reads the files it needs from your own installed copy of the game.

* three.js r160 with OrbitControls (MIT) -- https://threejs.org -- web/vendor/three/LICENSE
* retoc (MIT) -- https://github.com/trumank/retoc -- tools/retoc-LICENSE
